# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone participating in this project, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and project goals.
- Respect privacy, especially around reasoning traces and local experiment data.
- Accept maintainer decisions and disengage when asked.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.
Use GitHub's report-content tools for abusive public content. For a private
project-specific concern, contact the project owner using the contact
information on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/CedricCaruzzo).
Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Enforcement decisions will aim to protect affected people, preserve relevant
evidence, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and apply these standards consistently.
